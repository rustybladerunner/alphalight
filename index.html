AN EARLY EXPERIMENT

Alphalight

A small browser light experiment. Start deliberately. Stop at any time.

Flashing-light warning

Do not use this experiment if you have epilepsy or sensitivity to flickering light. Stop immediately if you feel unwell. This is not a medical device or treatment.

The page starts still. Playback is disabled when your device requests reduced motion.

Stopped. The light stays still until you press Start.

Escape stops playback. Changing frequency or leaving the tab also stops it.

10 Hz and 40 Hz are requested timer rates, not measured light output. Screen refresh, browser timing, and hardware can change what appears. No health benefit has been established here.
